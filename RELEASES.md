# Releases

## v1.0.0 — 24 August 2026

This is **v1.0** of Player Insights Agent (Astrolabe): the product that shipped
over the last few days.

What a reader gets now:

- Ask, Monitoring, Connections, Architecture and Run Explorer sit on one night
  sky. Live work stays in the answer corner.
- An answer says what it is. A successful table listing is Complete, not
  refused. A words-only reply is Partial, not a grant denial. Empty or
  deadline-stopped runs are not finished answers. The identity and row-filter
  lecture is gone from Keep in mind.
- Cost is attributed per component and is not totalled. The conversation rail
  badges every question, including someone else's.
- Monitoring loads its list once and keeps it when you leave the tab.
- Light mode is all or nothing: the agent-map band and page sky go light
  when Dark is off. No leftover night surfaces.
- Every number still comes from a live query under the asker's grants, and
  every run leaves a trace.

Tag: `v1.0.0`. Internal `origin` is the source of truth; the public mirror
carries the same note.
